# 🌌 BEYOND

Made in **C++**, heres what the games introduces:

> **Build the rocket. Leave Earth. Discover a universe that has never existed before.**

**BEYOND** is a free-roam space exploration game built around one idea:

# **What if every world you discovered was truly different?**

You begin on **Earth** with no spaceship, no interplanetary technology, and no predetermined destiny.

You must find your own way to build — or obtain — a rocket.

Once you leave Earth, the game changes completely.

You enter a procedurally generated universe containing an enormous number of unique planets, civilizations, ecosystems, environments, technologies, structures, resources, and unexplained phenomena.

There is no single path through the game.

There is no required career.

There is no fixed destination.

**You decide what kind of explorer you become.**

# 🌌 Game Overview

BEYOND is not designed around a traditional linear campaign.

Instead, it is built around **exploration and player-created experiences**.

You begin on Earth.

You gather resources.

You learn how technology works.

You construct your first spacecraft.

You launch.

And suddenly, the entire universe becomes available to you.

From that point onward, the game is about **discovery**.

You might spend hours exploring a single planet.

You might become obsessed with finding rare resources.

You might build an enormous base.

You might trade with alien civilizations.

You might search for ancient ruins.

You might simply fly from planet to planet and see what exists.

There is no "correct" way to play.

---

# 🎮 Core Gameplay

The main gameplay loop is:

```text
EXPLORE
   ↓
DISCOVER
   ↓
COLLECT
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
BUILD
   ↓
UPGRADE
   ↓
TRAVEL
   ↓
DISCOVER SOMETHING NEW
   ↓
REPEAT
```

But the loop isn't restrictive.

At almost any point, the player can change direction.

For example:

```text
Earth
 ↓
Build rocket
 ↓
Discover planet
 ↓
Find rare material
 ↓
Build base
 ↓
Meet civilization
 ↓
Trade
 ↓
Buy better spacecraft
 ↓
Leave
 ↓
Discover completely different planet
```

Or:

```text
Earth
 ↓
Build rocket
 ↓
Explore
 ↓
Find beautiful planet
 ↓
Build a house
 ↓
Stay there
```

Both are valid.

# 🌍 Starting on Earth

BEYOND begins on **Earth**.

Earth is intentionally familiar.

This gives the player a recognizable starting point before the game begins introducing increasingly strange environments.

At the beginning, you have limited technology.

You cannot simply open a menu and select a planet.

You need a way to leave Earth.

### Early gameplay can involve:

- Gathering materials
- Finding useful technology
- Learning about propulsion
- Building machinery
- Producing energy
- Creating fuel
- Finding or manufacturing components
- Constructing a workshop
- Building infrastructure
- Researching spacecraft technology

The player gradually progresses from:

**ordinary human → engineer → astronaut → explorer.**

The first rocket is one of the most important moments in the game.

It shouldn't simply be:

Instead, the player should feel like they **earned their way into space**.

The player may need to acquire:

### 🔩 Structural components

- Hull
- Frame
- Fuel tanks
- Control systems
- Landing systems

### ⚡ Electrical components

- Batteries
- Generators
- Computers
- Navigation systems

### 🔥 Propulsion

- Engines
- Fuel
- Ignition systems
- Thrust control

### 🛰️ Navigation

- Sensors
- Communication systems
- Navigation computer

The player can gradually improve their technology.

The first rocket may be unreliable.

Later spacecraft can become dramatically more capable.

# 🌌 Leaving Earth

The first launch is the moment the game truly opens.

# ♾️ Procedural Generation

Procedural generation is not used simply to make the universe large.

It is used to create **variety**.

The generation system can combine many layers:

```text
GALAXY
   ↓
STAR
   ↓
PLANET
   ↓
ENVIRONMENT
   ↓
CLIMATE
   ↓
TERRAIN
   ↓
BIOLOGY
   ↓
CIVILIZATION
   ↓
ARCHITECTURE
   ↓
NPCs
   ↓
ECONOMY
   ↓
HISTORY
   ↓
UNIQUE EVENTS
```

The goal is for these systems to influence one another.

For example:

**Planet has high gravity**

↓

Creatures evolve differently.

↓

Buildings need stronger structures.

↓

Vehicles need different designs.

↓

Civilization develops different technology.

↓

The planet feels different from another world.

# 🧠 The Philosophy

BEYOND is built around a simple philosophy:

> ### **Don't make the player ask "What mission should I do?"**
>
> ### **Make them ask "What's over there?"**

Curiosity should be the primary motivation.

The player should constantly wonder:

**What's beyond that mountain?**

**What's on that planet?**

**Who built that structure?**

**Why is the sky that color?**

**What is that creature?**

**What happens if I go there?**

**What is beyond this galaxy?**

# 📜 Development Principles

BEYOND follows several core rules.

### 1. **Never sacrifice variety for quantity**

A billion planets that all feel identical aren't useful.

### 2. **Every world should have an identity**

A planet should feel memorable.

### 3. **The player should have freedom**

Avoid forcing players into one playstyle.

### 4. **Discovery should matter**

Finding something new should feel exciting.

### 5. **The universe shouldn't revolve around the player**

NPCs and civilizations should have their own lives.

### 6. **The unknown should stay unknown**

Don't explain everything immediately.

### 7. **Systems should interact**

The best moments should sometimes happen without being explicitly scripted.
